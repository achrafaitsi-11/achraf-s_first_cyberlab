# Cisco Notes

## CIA Triad

### Confidentiality
Protect information from unauthorized access.

Examples:

- Passwords
- Two-factor authentication
- Encryption

### Integrity

Ensure information remains accurate.

Examples:

- Hashing
- Digital signatures

### Availability

Ensure systems remain accessible.

Examples:

- Backups
- Redundancy

## Attacker Types

### White hat

Authorized ethical hackers.

### Gray hat

Unauthorized but not malicious.

### Black hat

Malicious attackers.
